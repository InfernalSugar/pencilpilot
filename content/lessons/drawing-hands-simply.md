## Start with a mitten

Hands look hard because there are so many parts. So do not start with the parts. Start with the **mitten**: one shape for the palm and all four fingers together, and a second, smaller shape for the thumb.

The mitten captures the gesture of the hand: whether it is open or closed, which way it points, and how big it is. Check its size against the face: an adult hand, from wrist to fingertip, is roughly as long as the face from chin to hairline. If the mitten is the right size and pointing the right way, half the job is done.

## The palm box

Inside the mitten, draw the palm as a **box**, close to square. It is thicker at the thumb side and thinner at the little-finger side, which matters as soon as the hand turns.

Here is the proportion that surprises most people: the fingers are long. The middle finger is roughly as long as the palm itself. Most beginners draw the palm too big and the fingers too short, which makes the hand look like a paw.

## Fingers as tubes

Each finger is a **tube** with three segments, each segment a little shorter than the one before it. The middle finger is longest, the ring and index fingers are close to each other in length, and the little finger is clearly shorter, with its base set lower on the palm.

That last point is why the knuckles sit on a **curve**, not a straight line. Draw a gentle arc across the top of the palm box and start each finger from it. The same arc repeats at every joint, so a relaxed hand has fingertips that also form a curve.

## The thumb is different

The thumb does not grow out of the side of the fingers. It starts low, near the wrist, with a big fleshy pad at its base that is part of the palm. From there it has two visible segments and points away from the fingers at an angle.

Draw it as a wedge from the wrist side of the palm, then the two tubes. When the hand closes, the thumb swings across the palm instead of folding down like a finger. Once the mitten, box, tubes and thumb all sit right, round off the corners and add the creases. Leave out the fingernails until the very end, if at all.
